# MXRP Ragdoll — Roadmap

Features planned for future versions of MXRP Ragdoll. This list will grow over time.

## Planned

- [ ] **VR view while walking** — play the on-foot part in VR.
- [ ] **Refuelling system** — refuel your bike during sessions.
- [ ] **Emotes** — animations for your rider on foot.
- [ ] **Combat** — fight other riders on foot.
- [ ] **Ramps at any height** — ramps rideable even above the highest point of the track.
- [ ] **More objects** — new track objects to place, beyond cones, hay bales and ramps.
- [ ] **Overall improvements** — better animations, physics and performance across the whole plugin.

## Done

- [x] Physics ragdoll on crash, natural get-up
- [x] Walk, run, jump; lift the bike and get back on
- [x] Camera follows the rider on foot
- [x] Online play on RAGDOLL servers, player collisions, auto-kick of players without the plugin (Beta 2)
- [x] Cones and hay bales, shared online (Beta 2.1)
- [x] Performance fixes for objects (Beta 2.2)
- [x] FMX ramps, Xbox / PlayStation button symbols, tutorial, automatic language, support for the game update of 23 Nov 2026 (Beta 3)
